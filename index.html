Who We Are

About Ganesha Foundation

Empowering People, Protecting Rights and Liberties

Ganesha Foundation is a social service organization registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Act 21). It is committed to promoting civil rights and liberties guaranteed under the Constitution of India among the people of Bihar.

The foundation works to protect individuals from unfair exploitation by those in power and challenges policies that increase the suffering of common people. It also strives to reduce social and personal conflicts arising from inequality, ego clashes, and lack of human values.

Through awareness, legal assistance, and community engagement, the organization aims to create a more just, peaceful, and empowered society.

Our Vision

Key Reforms & Issues that Citizens shall Work on Collectively

Uprising focuses on bold structural reforms aimed at building a transparent, fair, and people-driven governance system.

Tax Reform

Simplifying taxation by replacing Income Tax and GST with a single, transparent Banking Transaction Tax system.

  • • Max 1% transaction-based tax
  • • Reduce corruption & complexity
  • • Cash limit capped at ₹2500

Political Reform

Ensuring equality and accountability among elected representatives.

  • • Fixed decent salary for leaders
  • • No luxury benefits or privileges
  • • Limited official vehicle use

Administrative Reform

Building a lean, efficient, and accountable bureaucracy.

  • • Salary-based structure only
  • • Limited perks (TA/DA only)
  • • No financial power concentration

Population Policy

Encouraging responsible family planning for sustainable growth.

  • • One-child policy proposal
  • • 5-year gap before first child
  • • Minimum marriage age: 25

Green Economy

Promoting eco-friendly industries and sustainable agriculture.

  • • Only pollution-free industries
  • • Mandatory organic farming
  • • Government-backed procurement

Judicial Reform

Decentralizing justice to empower local governance systems.

  • • Strengthen Gram Kachehries
  • • Faster dispute resolution
  • • Reduce court burden

Police Reform

Creating a transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly policing system.

  • • Accountability mechanisms
  • • Reduced misuse of power
  • • Citizen-first approach

People’s Participation

Making governance inclusive through active civil society involvement.

  • • Mandatory public consultation
  • • Civil society in policymaking
  • • Transparent implementation
Together, these reforms aim to build a fair, transparent, and future-ready Bihar.

Your Courage is Your Destiny

Rise, Awake, Get Up on Your Feet

Bihar Awaits for the Long Pending Change

Democracy is fundamentally a game of numbers. The greater your numbers, the greater your collective strength. The only essential condition, however, is ethics. If ethics are compromised, victory may still be possible, but it will only be temporary. If one stands firmly by ethics, the victory becomes comprehensive and enduring.

The most important question, therefore, is: where do these numbers come from in support of any ethical cause?

Ganesha Foundation, through its initiative "This is an Uprising…", offers the citizens of Bihar an opportunity to come forward and build that critical numerical strength through dialogue, discussions, and eventually collective action. Such collective participation can create the necessary pressure to move institutions in favor of both the individual and the community.

Since independence, a narrative has been allowed to prevail that the people of this country are the true masters. However, through constitutional maneuvering, the reality appears to be quite the opposite. Ironically, those who were meant to serve the nation have gradually overwhelmed the spirit of independent India and assumed powers to rule over the common people rather than serve them. Countless examples from the lives of ordinary citizens reveal the painful truth that, in reality, they are often treated as little more than pariahs.

What we have today in the name of governance, under the borrowed slogan of "by the people, of the people, and for the people," often feels far removed from its true spirit. Instead of institutions serving the common citizen, many people feel burdened by systems that appear to favor a handful of powerful politicians, bureaucrats, and their associates. The widespread sense of suffocation and helplessness among ordinary Indians stands as testimony to this reality.

This naturally leads to the most legitimate question: what is the solution?

The answer is simple — come together. Know the people around you, understand their suffering, and recognize that their struggles are often shared by your own. The greater the numbers, the stronger the collective voice, and the more likely it is to be heard.

Ganesha Foundation seeks to become one such platform committed to bringing people together, setting aside personal conflicts and differences, and transforming unity into a practical reality. Membership is the first step in this direction, strengthening the platform both financially and numerically so that collective action can be undertaken in the interest of society.

Lastly, the youth of this country, by leading increasingly individualistic lives, risk undermining their own well-being and exposing themselves to exploitation, oppression, and diminished existence. It is imperative that they come forward and embrace the responsibility of continuous vigilance through collective participation in governance processes. To achieve this, they should organize themselves and become active members of platforms dedicated to civil rights and liberties, of which Ganesha Foundation could be one.

The sense of purpose, fulfillment, and joy that comes from participating in the nation-building process is truly unparalleled. The question now is — are you ready?

उठो, जागो, अपने पाँवों पर खड़े हो जाओ — बिहार लंबे समय से परिवर्तन की राह देख रहा है।

लोकतंत्र मूलतः संख्या का खेल है। जितनी अधिक संख्या, उतनी अधिक सामूहिक शक्ति। किंतु इसकी सबसे महत्वपूर्ण शर्त है — नैतिकता। यदि नैतिकता से समझौता किया जाता है, तो विजय संभव तो है, परंतु वह केवल अस्थायी होगी। यदि नैतिकता के साथ दृढ़ता से खड़े रहा जाए, तो विजय व्यापक और स्थायी होती है।

सबसे महत्वपूर्ण प्रश्न यह है कि किसी भी नैतिक मुद्दे के समर्थन में यह संख्या कहाँ से आएगी?

Ganesha Foundation अपनी पहल "This is an Uprising…" के माध्यम से बिहार के नागरिकों को आगे आने और संवाद, विचार-विमर्श तथा अंततः सामूहिक कार्रवाई के माध्यम से उस निर्णायक संख्या को प्राप्त करने का अवसर प्रदान करता है। ऐसी सामूहिक भागीदारी व्यक्ति और समुदाय, दोनों के हित में व्यवस्था को प्रभावित करने हेतु आवश्यक दबाव बना सकती है।

स्वतंत्रता के बाद से यह धारणा फैलायी जाती रही है कि इस देश के लोग ही इसके वास्तविक स्वामी हैं। किंतु संवैधानिक प्रक्रियाओं और व्यवस्थागत बदलावों के माध्यम से वास्तविकता इसके विपरीत प्रतीत होती है। यह एक विडंबना है कि जिनका दायित्व जनता की सेवा करना था, उन्होंने स्वतंत्र भारत की मूल भावना को दबा दिया और सेवा करने के बजाय आम लोगों पर शासन करने की शक्तियाँ अपने हाथों में केंद्रित कर लीं। आम नागरिकों के जीवन से असंख्य उदाहरण इस कटु सत्य को उजागर करते हैं कि वास्तव में उनकी स्थिति उपेक्षित और परित्यक्त लोगों जैसी हो गई है।

आज शासन और संविधान के नाम पर "जनता का, जनता के द्वारा, जनता के लिए" जैसे उधार लिए गए आदर्श वाक्य की वास्तविक आत्मा आम नागरिकों के अनुभव से मेल नहीं खाती। अनेक लोगों को यह अनुभव होता है कि व्यवस्थाएँ जनता की सेवा करने के बजाय कुछ शक्तिशाली राजनेताओं, नौकरशाहों और उनके सहयोगियों के हितों की पूर्ति में अधिक सक्रिय हैं। अधिकांश भारतीयों के जीवन में व्याप्त घुटन और असहायता इसी वास्तविकता का प्रमाण है।

स्वाभाविक रूप से सबसे वैध प्रश्न उठता है — समाधान क्या है?

उत्तर अत्यंत सरल है — एक साथ आइए। अपने आसपास के लोगों को जानिए, उनके कष्टों को समझिए, और यह पहचानिए कि उनका संघर्ष कहीं न कहीं आपका भी संघर्ष है। जितनी अधिक संख्या होगी, उतनी ही अधिक आपकी आवाज़ सुनी जाएगी।

Ganesha Foundation ऐसा ही एक मंच बनने के लिए प्रतिबद्ध है, जो व्यक्तिगत मतभेदों और टकरावों को एक ओर रखकर लोगों को एकजुट करने का कार्य करता है। सदस्यता ग्रहण करना इस दिशा में पहला कदम है, जो मंच को संख्या और आर्थिक शक्ति दोनों प्रदान करता है, ताकि सामूहिक कार्रवाई समाज और नागरिक हितों में की जा सके।

अंततः, इस देश का युवा वर्ग अत्यधिक व्यक्तिवादी जीवन जीते हुए अपने समग्र अस्तित्व को क्षति पहुँचा रहा है और स्वयं को शोषण, उत्पीड़न तथा अमानवीय जीवन-स्थितियों के जोखिम में डाल रहा है। अब समय आ गया है कि वे आगे आएँ और शासन-प्रक्रियाओं में सामूहिक भागीदारी के माध्यम से निरंतर सतर्कता की जिम्मेदारी स्वीकार करें। इसके लिए उन्हें नागरिक अधिकारों और स्वतंत्रताओं के लिए कार्यरत मंचों से जुड़ना चाहिए, जिनमें Ganesha Foundation एक मंच हो सकता है।

राष्ट्र-निर्माण की प्रक्रिया में भाग लेने का आनंद, संतोष और उद्देश्यबोध वास्तव में अनुपम है। अब प्रश्न केवल इतना है — क्या आप तैयार हैं?

Your Rights Under the Constitution

Every citizen of India has important rights given by the Constitution (Part III – Articles 12 to 35). These rights protect your freedom, ensure equality, and give you the power to take action when treated unfairly.

1) Civil Liberties

Civil liberties are the freedoms protected from arbitrary interference by the State.

  • • Freedom of speech and expression
  • • Freedom of movement
  • • Freedom of assembly
  • • Freedom of association
  • • Freedom of religion
  • • Personal liberty and life

(Articles 19 & 21)

Example: You can express your opinion freely, move anywhere in India, protest peacefully, and live with dignity.

2) Civil Rights

Civil rights focus on equality, protection from discrimination, and fair treatment under the law.

  • • Equality before law (Article 14)
  • • Prohibition of discrimination based on religion, race, caste, sex, or place of birth (Article 15)
  • • Equal opportunity in public employment (Article 16)
  • • Abolition of untouchability (Article 17)
  • • Protection against exploitation (Articles 23–24)

These rights ensure that every citizen is treated with equality, dignity, and justice.

3) Constitutional Remedies

Article 32 gives you the power to approach the Supreme Court or High Court if your rights are violated.

It is called the “heart and soul of the Constitution” because it allows citizens to fight for justice.
