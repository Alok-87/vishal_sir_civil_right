About Ganesha Foundation

Empowering People, Protecting Rights

Ganesha Foundation is a social service organization registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Act 21). It is committed to promoting civil rights and liberties guaranteed under the Constitution of India among the people of Bihar.

The foundation works to protect individuals from unfair exploitation by those in power and challenges policies that increase the suffering of common people. It also strives to reduce social and personal conflicts arising from inequality, ego clashes, and lack of human values.

Through awareness, legal support, and community engagement, the organization aims to create a more just, peaceful, and empowered society.

Legal Support

Filing PILs against harmful policies and providing free legal aid to protect citizens' rights.

Peace & Harmony

Promoting social harmony and resolving conflicts through collective community efforts.

Awareness Programs

Conducting campaigns to educate people about their constitutional rights and freedoms.

Social Protection

Supporting vulnerable individuals against exploitation and dominance by powerful groups.

Bihari's Call to Rebuild Bihar

Common Man Uprising

A people-driven initiative dedicated to promoting civil rights, constitutional awareness, and justice for every citizen of Bihar through collective action and empowerment.

"Common Man Uprising is a movement to empower citizens with awareness, courage, and unity to stand against injustice and protect their constitutional rights."

— Ganesha Foundation
  1. Awakening of Rights
    Many citizens remain unaware of the rights and liberties guaranteed under the Constitution of India. This initiative focuses on spreading awareness so individuals can recognize injustice and stand up for their dignity and freedom.
  2. Standing Against Exploitation
    The movement aims to protect people from unfair exploitation by those in positions of power. It encourages collective voice and legal action against policies or practices that harm the public.
  3. Building a Just Society
    Through awareness, legal support, and community participation, the initiative works toward reducing conflicts, promoting human values, and creating a peaceful and equitable society.

Legal & Awareness Action

  • Filing Public Interest Litigations (PILs) against harmful policies
  • Providing free legal aid to protect individual rights
  • Conducting awareness programs on constitutional rights
  • Supporting citizens in raising their voice against injustice

Vision for Society

  • Promoting peace and harmony across communities
  • Reducing conflicts caused by inequality and misuse of power
  • Encouraging active citizen participation in governance
  • Building a society based on justice, dignity, and equality
